## Behavior added / repaired

### Exact packet / claim binding

A packet cannot become operational through logical-id, newest/current, storage-recency, or array-order lookup. The exact `claim_ref` embedded in the packet is loaded directly.

### No silent handoff rebase

`packet.base_state_revision_ref` must equal the exact referenced claim's `base_state_revision_ref`. A later state revision cannot silently substitute for the work base the packet is returning from.

### Lane consistency and historical relationship reconstruction

The packet's `lane_id` must equal the exact claim's logical lane. Exact claim existence alone is not treated as proof that its historical relationships were previously admitted correctly. Before operational packet use, the runtime reconstructs:

- the claim's exact base / exactly-one lane relation;
- the claim's exact occupancy relation;
- the occupancy's own exact historical entry-base / exactly-one lane relation;
- occupancy/claim persistent logical-lane equality.

This preserves Decision 007 chronology: occupancy entry base may differ from the later claim/packet base.

### Local lifecycle prerequisites remain local

The exact claim snapshot must say `open`; the exact occupancy snapshot must say `active`. These are necessary local properties only. The runtime does not relabel them as global currentness, unsuperseded state, authorization, scheduler authority, scope ownership, or lock ownership.

### Proof-to-write continuity

The caller's packet is deep-copied before validation or dependency grounding. The same function-owned detached candidate is persisted after proof, so caller mutation at the final write boundary cannot change the packet whose relationships were grounded.

### Handoff fields preserved without invented closure

`changes`, `artifacts_created`, `artifacts_modified`, `evidence_refs`, `uncertainties`, `failures_or_blockers`, `downstream_effects`, and `requested_followup` are preserved exactly through immutable persistence. Their presence is not treated as proof of artifact provenance, artifact compatibility, evidence validity/closure, lane output compatibility, or integration acceptance.
