```text
snapshot one function-owned detached work-claim candidate
-> validate the exact candidate against work-claim.schema.json
-> require local claim status == open
-> exact-load base_state_revision_ref as state-revision
-> resolve lane_id exactly once from that exact base's lane_refs

A regression demonstrates a later claim base may bind the same exact occupancy while resolving the same persistent logical lane, preserving Decision 007 chronology rather than inventing a stricter rule.

`occupancy.claim_ids` and `work-claim.overlap_with_claim_ids` remain stored snapshot/reporting data only. They are not dereference, ownership, locking, or arbitration authority in this slice.
