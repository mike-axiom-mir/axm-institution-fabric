### ADV-034-A — same logical revision-id decoy cannot silently rebase packet

The fixture first creates a valid claim against one exact claim base. It then stores another state revision with the **same logical revision id** but different exact content and a different exact same-logical-id lane instance.

Expected invariant:

- the packet remains bound to the exact claim base named by its exact `base_state_revision_ref`;
- the returned lane remains the exact lane selected by that exact claim base;
- the later same-logical-id revision/lane cannot acquire newest/current/recent authority.

### ADV-034-B — ambiguous occupancy entry relation cannot be laundered by packet admission

The fixture directly stores an active occupancy whose exact entry base contains two exact lane instances sharing the occupancy's persistent logical lane id. A clean later claim is then direct-stored against a separate unambiguous claim base.

Expected invariant:

- exact claim/occupancy object presence is insufficient;
- packet admission must re-ground the occupancy's own historical entry-base relationship;
- the ambiguous relation must fail explicitly before any packet is persisted.

### ADV-034-C — later closed snapshots cannot gain recency/currentness authority

The fixture creates one valid exact `open` claim bound to one exact `active` occupancy. It then stores later immutable snapshots with the same logical ids: an `ended` occupancy and a `submitted` claim.

Expected invariant:

- the packet's exact `claim_ref` continues to select the original exact open claim;
- that claim's exact `occupancy_ref` continues to select the original exact active occupancy;
- storage order, apparent lifecycle recency, or a later same-id status cannot replace exact references.

This is intentionally **not** a claim that the older snapshots are globally current. It proves only that this bounded transition does not silently infer currentness from recency.

### ADV-034-D — nested proof-to-write caller aliasing

A custom store mutates the caller's already-existing nested handoff arrays only at the final return-packet publication boundary:

- appends a new caller-only uncertainty;
- clears the caller's evidence-ref array.

Expected invariant:

- the durable packet remains exactly the detached function-owned candidate that was grounded before publication;
- the late caller mutation cannot silently rewrite durable uncertainty/evidence content;
- exact claim/occupancy/lane relationships remain unchanged.
