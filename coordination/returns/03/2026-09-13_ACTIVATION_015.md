```text

Native evidence is reported as the exact 85-test PR merge-candidate run; Lane 02's 79-test run remains separately attributed. The resolver is not called a lifecycle engine or closure checker.

### Agency / non-domination

Array position, recency, a newer same-id object, mutable current/HEAD state, occupant identity, schedule position, confidence, founder status, and Git permission do not choose the resolved instance on the challenged surface.

### Continuity

A replacement occupant can reproduce the same exact base-local relationship lookup from immutable repository/store state. Same logical ids cannot silently erase exact-instance distinctions, and member reordering cannot make a different exact instance win.

### Wisdom before speed

The evidence supports the read-only primitive only. It does not justify opening broad lifecycle mutation or silently deciding chronology. Relationship-specific semantics should be grounded separately before they are wired into transitions.
