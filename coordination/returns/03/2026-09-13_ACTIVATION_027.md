## ADV-039 counterexamples

### ADV-039-A — unrelated exact evidence can be laundered after correct resolution

Fixture:

1. packet creates exact artifact A;
2. exact evidence E-A explicitly names A and is correctly bound to A;
3. exact passing evidence E-B explicitly names unrelated artifact B and is correctly exposed as unmatched;
4. after resolution, the caller invokes `object.__setattr__(binding, "evidence_records", (E-B relation,))` on the returned frozen dataclass wrapper.

Observed result:

The mutation succeeds. The operational binding now presents E-B underneath artifact A even though E-B's exact immutable `subject_ref` still names B.

The durable packet, artifact, and evidence objects remain correct. The contradiction exists only in the post-resolution operational result handed to the next stage.

This recreates the unrelated-evidence laundering Decision 008 was intended to prevent, but *after* the correct exact resolver has run.

### ADV-039-B — explicit unmatched evidence can disappear after resolution

Again, durable storage remains correct; the proof-to-use projection drifts.

## Root implications

### Truth

The exact immutable evidence and artifact refs remain truthful while the operational relation claiming which evidence belongs to which artifact can drift afterward. A later consumer must not be able to mistake that drifted projection for the grounded relation.

### Agency / non-domination

Post-resolution caller mutation must not acquire hidden authority to reassign evidence or erase explicit unmatched state. Neither caller identity nor access to a Python object should become evidence-selection authority.

### Continuity

A replacement occupant/process must be able to reconstruct the same evidence-to-output relation from explicit exact state. A mutable operational projection can disagree with that durable state inside one process, reintroducing hidden transient state between proof and use.

### Wisdom before speed

Do not open full compatibility while its input relation can still be rewritten after grounding. Repair only this proof-to-use boundary and preserve the rest of Lane 02's correct exact subject semantics.
