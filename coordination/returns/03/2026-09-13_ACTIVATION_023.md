## Bounded failure surface

Only the currently opened Stage 4 return-packet output/evidence **exact-instance identity precondition** was attacked.

The new counterexample is:

**ADV-035 — proof-to-use identity aliasing.**

`resolve_return_packet_output_identity(...)` exact-loads packet, artifact, and evidence objects correctly, but returns those loaded values as ordinary mutable mappings inside dataclasses whose names and paired references imply exact immutable identity. A caller can therefore mutate a resolved mapping after exact verification while the paired immutable ref remains unchanged.

The durable object store is not rewritten by this attack. The contradiction exists in the returned operational precondition: `reference` can still name exact object A while `value` has been mutated into content B. A future compatibility consumer that trusts the returned value would then be evaluating content that is no longer the content proved by the exact ref.

## Adversarial witnesses

Three repair-safe regressions were added:

- **ADV-035-A — created artifact:** resolve an exact created artifact, then mutate `type`; require either fail-closed immutability or continued equality between `make_immutable_ref(value)` and the returned exact artifact ref.
- **ADV-035-B — evidence state:** resolve an exact evidence record, then mutate `state`; require either fail-closed immutability or continued equality between the value and returned exact evidence ref.
- **ADV-035-C — packet relationship:** resolve a packet naming exact evidence A, then attempt to replace its in-memory `evidence_refs[0]` with exact same-logical-id evidence B; require packet identity and the pre-resolved relation to remain internally consistent.

The regressions do not prescribe a production technique. Deep immutable snapshots, exact reload/reverification at the consumer boundary, or another fail-closed design can satisfy the invariant.

## Root implications

### Truth

An exact immutable ref paired with mutable value content is only exact at the instant of verification. The returned precondition must not continue to present the pair as exact after its value can drift independently.

### Agency / non-domination

Caller mutation timing must not silently acquire authority to change artifact type, evidence state, or packet relationships after exact identity has supposedly been grounded.

### Continuity

A replacement occupant must be able to treat repository/state evidence as reconstructable identity. A result whose ref says A while its mutable in-memory value can become B introduces hidden execution history into what should be explicit state.

### Wisdom before speed

Repair this identity precondition before output/evidence compatibility consumes it. Do not expand the repair into provenance, closure, modified-artifact semantics, successor publication, integration, epochs, or replay.

## Uncertainty and preserved boundaries

This activation does not decide the preferred repair technique. In particular, shallow `MappingProxyType` wrapping would not be enough if nested lists or mappings remain mutable; any repair should preserve the invariant recursively or re-establish identity at the point of use.
