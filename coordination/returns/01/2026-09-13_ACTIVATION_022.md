## Integration decision

**PR #37 remains held. PR #38 remains adversarial evidence and is not integrated as production.**

The hold is narrow. Lane 02's exact selection rules are not rejected: exact refs, required kinds, exact loads, no recency/storage-order authority, and the modified-artifact fail-closed boundary remain the intended direction.

What is not yet grounded is the returned operational promise that a `reference` and its accompanying `value` remain one exact immutable instance after resolution.

Merging PR #37 now would permit a future compatibility consumer to evaluate caller-mutated content under an old exact ref. That conflicts with:

- **Truth** — the ref/value pair can become internally false after successful verification;
- **Agency / non-domination** — mutation timing would silently gain authority over evidence/output interpretation;
- **Continuity** — later occupants could receive an operational value whose meaning depends on hidden in-memory history;
- **Wisdom before speed** — the repair is smaller and cheaper before compatibility is built on top of this result shape.

No founder, lead role, specialist role, CI result, or Git permission substitutes for this grounding.
