Inspection of `state-revision.schema.json` against integrated Decision 002 / Stage 2 identity semantics exposed a cross-object continuity gap.

The current v0.1 revision schema binds these relationships only by logical id:

- `parent_revision_id`;
- `objective_id`;
- `lane_ids`;
- `occupancy_ids`;
- `claim_ids`;
- `return_packet_ids`;
- `integration_receipt_ids`.

But Stage 2 now explicitly proves that logical identity and immutable instance identity are different concepts.

Concrete witness:

- objective A: `id = objective.alpha`, desired outcome X;
- objective B: `id = objective.alpha`, desired outcome Y;
- both may be valid objects with different canonical hashes / immutable refs;
- a revision containing only `objective_id = objective.alpha` cannot identify which exact objective instance composes the snapshot.

Persisting such a revision as a canonical immutable state would require an undocumented mutable-id convention and would not be self-sufficient for replay.
