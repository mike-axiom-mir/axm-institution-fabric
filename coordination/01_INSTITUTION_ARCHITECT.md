## Integration discipline

For every meaningful change, ask:

- What explicit state changed?
- Which invariant or objective does it serve?
- What evidence supports the claim?
- What uncertainty remains?
- What dependencies or downstream artifacts are affected?
- Is the change universal-kernel behavior or domain-package behavior?
- Can the transition be replayed/explained?
- Does integration remain compatible with Truth, Agency/non-domination, Continuity, and Wisdom before speed?

If the answer is unresolved, preserve the unresolved state instead of silently converting confidence or role seniority into authority.
